# Code of Conduct

## In short

We discuss the work, not the people. Disagreeing about design is normal;
making it personal is not.

## What is expected

- Specifics instead of verdicts: "this rule is not checkable, here is case
  X" beats "this rule is bad".
- Respect for context you cannot see: the author of a rule may have had
  constraints you do not know about. Ask.
- Language tolerance: issues in any language are translated, not closed.
- Admitting a mistake is a normal part of the work, not a defeat.

## What is unacceptable

- Insults, belittling, harassment - including "as a joke".
- Discrimination on any grounds.
- Publishing other people's personal data.
- Pushing after a clear "no" from the maintainer.
- Posting data obtained under NDA - yours or anyone else's.

## Reporting

Message the maintainer through GitHub's private channel (Security ->
Report a vulnerability works for this too when public discussion would be
inappropriate). Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that
do not follow this code, giving a reason; repeated violations may lead to
restricted participation.

## Basis

Adapted and condensed from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
